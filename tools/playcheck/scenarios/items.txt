# Items in the own player's hand (M4-8, #276) in the game's real windows. Window 1 hosts and is
# forced to be crew, window 2 joins as the dissident; four knives lie on the greybox floor. Window
# 1 starts with an empty Hand and Belt, faces the nearest knife (`aim item knife`: the mouse is
# never captured, so this is how a window turns) and walks to it until the HUD names it under the
# crosshair (Aim, #489), picks it up (E) and sees it in its first-person hand; swaps it to the belt
# (the hand empty) and back, then puts it down. Window 2 sees its own role.
# The text waits read the Toy HUD's English words (#489): a slot shows its item's name.
players 2
windows 2
role 1 crew
role 2 dissident
setting knives 4

window 1
wait phase lobby
press ready
wait screen round timeout=60
wait text hud.role is Engineer
wait shown hud.hand off
wait shown hud.belt off
wait shown hand.item off
shot round_hud_empty
# Picking up: face the nearest knife, walk until the HUD names it under the crosshair.
aim item knife
hold move_forward
wait text hud.aim is Knife timeout=30
release move_forward
# E goes at once: Aim stops a walking margin short of the host's reach (#319), so the host
# accepts it from the last claim it took. Stop aiming first (so the next knife does not turn the
# player once this one is held).
aim off
press interact
wait text hud.hand is Knife
wait text hand.item is knife
frames 30
shot knife_in_hand
# The swap key moves it to the belt, the hand empty, and back.
press swap
wait text hud.belt is Knife
wait shown hud.hand off
wait shown hand.item off
shot knife_on_belt
press swap
wait text hud.hand is Knife
press put_down
wait shown hud.hand off
wait shown hud.belt off
shot knife_put_down

window 2
wait phase lobby
press ready
wait screen round timeout=60
wait text hud.role is Dissident
frames 30
shot round_dissident_hud
