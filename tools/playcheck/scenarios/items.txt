# Items in the own player's hand (M4-8, #276) in the game's real windows. Window 1 hosts and is
# forced to be crew, window 2 joins as the dissident; four knives lie on the greybox floor. Window
# 1 starts with an empty Hand and Belt, faces the nearest knife (`aim item knife`: the mouse is
# never captured, so this is how a window turns) and walks to it until its pick-up hint shows,
# picks it up (E) and sees it in its first-person hand; swaps it to the belt (the hand empty) and
# back, then puts it down. Window 2 sees its own role.
# The text waits read short parts of the greybox wording (#150), never whole sentences.
players 2
windows 2
role 1 crew
role 2 dissident
setting knives 4

window 1
wait phase lobby
press ready
wait screen round timeout=60
wait text hud.role is Role: Engineer
wait text hud.hand is Hand: empty
wait text hud.belt is Belt: empty
wait shown hand.item off
shot round_hud_empty
# Picking up: face the nearest knife, walk until the crosshair's hint offers it.
aim item knife
hold move_forward
wait text hud.hint has pick up Knife timeout=30
release move_forward
# The hint shows at the edge of reach by the window's own position; the host checks the reach
# from the last claim it accepted (claims go at 20 Hz), which trails by up to a claim interval plus
# the latency (#319). Standing still lets it catch up: check the hint again, stop aiming (so the
# next knife does not turn the player once this one is held), then E.
frames 60
wait text hud.hint has pick up Knife
aim off
press interact
wait text hud.hand is Hand: Knife
wait text hand.item is knife
frames 30
shot knife_in_hand
# The swap key moves it to the belt, the hand empty, and back.
press swap
wait text hud.belt is Belt: Knife
wait text hud.hand is Hand: empty
wait shown hand.item off
shot knife_on_belt
press swap
wait text hud.hand is Hand: Knife
press put_down
wait text hud.hand is Hand: empty
wait text hud.belt is Belt: empty
shot knife_put_down

window 2
wait phase lobby
press ready
wait screen round timeout=60
wait text hud.role is Role: Dissident
frames 30
shot round_dissident_hud
