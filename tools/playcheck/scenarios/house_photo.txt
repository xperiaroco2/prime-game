# The photo camera's viewfinder on the House (#1105; photo P7, #786) in the game's real window.
# Window 1 hosts alone (the base mode plays with one) on the House. From the Yard it walks the
# route to the film box in the basement storage (`aim station film_box via to_film_box`: the
# waypoints through the doors and down the basement stairs, found by trial, then the box's use
# spot) until the HUD names the box under the crosshair, takes a film (E), walks back up and
# through the ground floor to the photo camera, loads the film (E) and taps E again: the
# viewfinder opens, its counter of frames left in the corner (shot). Then the left button (`use`,
# held: it has no key to press) takes a shot: the host's ShotTaken, and the counter one frame down
# (shot). The shot reads the game's pointer, which a playcheck window keeps without touching the
# real mouse (#1114). The pictures (P8, #787) are not on main yet.
# The waypoints are x,z in metres on the House's floor; the Yard's spawns lie around x 24-38,
# z 52-55, so the first one is reached straight from any of them. The House is a bench map since
# the M8 slice (#1496): `map` picks it, and `tasks` 5 deals its five task types, the photo task
# among them.
