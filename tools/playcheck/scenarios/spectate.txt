# room, #1489, in #1635) in the game's real windows. Window 1 hosts, window 2 joins, and player 3 is
# a bot forced to be the dissident (spectate_bots.tres): it takes a knife and knocks player 2 down,
# then player 1. Window 2 shows its downed camera with the Toy downed plates (#497: "You're down",

# dies. The round has a dead's hall, so the dead do not watch a living player through its eyes (the
# "Watching: <name>" line is only for a phase with no hall): window 2 shows the hall's DeadTop
# ("Back in m:ss") and none of the HUD, then comes back after the respawn time, protected.
