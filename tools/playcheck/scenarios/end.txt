# The end of a match and the next one (#276) in the game's real windows. Window 1 hosts and is
# forced to be the dissident; the match clock is forced to 20 s, and the time_up rule gives the
# dissidents the win when it ends with a task left. Both windows see the end screen; only the host
# has Back to lobby (its button, pressed with keys). In the lobby again nobody is ready
# (MatchState.reset_match on End -> Lobby), and both ready up for a second round. The forced clock
# is session state, so the second round ends after 20 s too: its shots come well before that.
# lobby_after_end is where #251 (a round snapshot drawn in the lobby after End) may show.
# The text waits read short parts of the greybox wording (#150), never whole sentences.
players 2
windows 2
role 1 dissident
clock 20

window 1
wait phase lobby
press ready
wait screen round timeout=60
wait text hud.clock has 0:
shot round_clock
wait screen end timeout=60
wait phase end
wait text end.winner is The Dissidents won
wait shown end.back on
wait pointer free
shot end_host
button Back to lobby
wait screen lobby timeout=30
wait ready off
wait ready 2 off
wait text lobby.countdown is Waiting for everyone
frames 60
shot lobby_after_end
press ready
wait screen round timeout=60
wait text hud.role has Role
shot second_round_host

window 2
wait phase lobby
press ready
wait screen round timeout=60
wait screen end timeout=60
wait text end.winner is The Dissidents won
wait shown end.back off
shot end_guest
wait screen lobby timeout=60
wait ready off
wait text lobby.roster has not ready

press ready
