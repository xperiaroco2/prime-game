# The solo tutorial session (#601) in the game's real window: --tutorial starts it without the
# invite, its host on a private in-process hub (no port), with the game's two stand-ins. gather
# has no level, so the loading screen shows until the room is in (the tutorial deals no task, so
# a tip, never a how-to card, #900); then the lessons phase shows the round in the tutorial room
# with three players in the roster, and #492's plates over the HUD (no timer, no role): lesson
# 1's row of keycaps (step-keys); a diagonal walk across the room (a second of walking, the
# lesson's placeholder, ends it well before a wall) brings lesson 2's sentence with its keycap
# (step). The package picked up and put down (lesson 2), then the knife picked up and swapped to the
# belt (lesson 3), brings lesson 4, the map: M opens it, with no task in its list (#900). Then the
# tutorial's Esc menu. The invite shows only on a first launch, which no playcheck window is
# (E70): its shots are client/dev/tutorial_*_preview.tscn's.
tutorial
timeout 90

window 1
wait screen loading
shot tutorial_loading
wait phase lessons
wait screen round
wait players 3
frames 30
wait text tutorial.step is Step 1 of 8
wait text tutorial.how has Walk
shot tutorial_room
hold move_forward
hold move_right
wait text tutorial.step is Step 2 of 8
release move_forward
release move_right
wait text tutorial.how has Aim at the package
frames 30
shot tutorial_step
# Lesson 2: face the package, walk until the HUD names it under the crosshair, pick it up, put
# it down.
aim item package
hold move_forward
wait text hud.aim is Package timeout=30
release move_forward
aim off
press interact
wait text hud.hand is Package
wait text tutorial.how has Press
press put_down
wait shown hud.hand off
wait text tutorial.step is Step 3 of 8
# Lesson 3: the knife (the package goes on no belt), then the swap key moves it to the belt.
aim item knife
hold move_forward
wait text hud.aim is Knife timeout=30
release move_forward
aim off
press interact
wait text hud.hand is Knife
press swap
wait text hud.belt is Knife
wait text tutorial.step is Step 4 of 8
frames 30
shot tutorial_map_step
# Lesson 4: M opens the map, its tasks list empty.
press map
wait pointer free
frames 30
shot tutorial_map
press map
wait pointer captured
wait text tutorial.step is Step 5 of 8
press ui_cancel
wait esc open
shot tutorial_esc
