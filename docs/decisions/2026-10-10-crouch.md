## Context

**What exists.** Nothing in `main`, `release/m7` or `release/m6.2` mentions a crouch (searched on 2026-10-10).
- The base mode's `PlayerRules` (placeholders): a capsule 1.8 m high with a 0.4 m radius, the eye at 1.6 m, the step
  at 0.3 m; walk 4.5 m/s, sprint 7.0 m/s, the crawl 1.0 m/s.
- `PlayerController` is a `CharacterBody3D` with its origin at the feet (ARCHITECTURE §7); `_apply_rules` builds its
  own `CapsuleShape3D` (never a shared resource), centred at half its height, and puts the head at the eye. A claim
  carries the flags `sprint`, `moving` and `on_floor` and the masks `sprint_ticks` and `moved_ticks` (ARCHITECTURE
  §4.3.2); the snapshot's avatar carries the flags `downed` and `invulnerable` (ARCHITECTURE §4.3.5).
- The host's movement checks read the feet: the floor under them, the rise above the last landing, the travel
  (ARCHITECTURE §7.1.4, §7.1.5). No capsule is checked against walls (ARCHITECTURE §7.1.9). The host reads a
  player's body in two places. The eye, `Items.eye_of` (the floor under the feet plus `eye_height_m`), starts the
  sight lines of `InSight`, `PutDownInFront`, `TargetInSight` (the raise) and `Strike`. The capsule's height sets
  `Strike`'s vertical overlap and the middle its sight line ends at (ARCHITECTURE §7.1.10), and on `release/m7` the
  throw's contact with a player (`FlightTicks`) and `ThrowItem`'s radius bound, `min(capsule_radius_m,
  capsule_height_m - eye_height_m)` less a margin.
- What others see and hear: remote bodies drawn from the snapshots' poses, their voice from a mouth below the eyes
  (`RemotePlayerBody.mouth_point`); and on `release/m6.2`, to reach `main` with it, name plates (#257) over the head
  (`NamePlates.plate_point`, over the drawn head), shown while one ray from the camera to the player's eye is clear,
  within 10 m (#756, M7 backlog, removes them: the engineer's KD2 answer); and footsteps (#525) from each
  body's interpolated motion, never a claimed flag, heard within 12 m (`SoundChooser.HEARING_RANGE_M`). Below the
  walk speed `FootstepCadence` holds the walk's stride (4.5 m/s times 0.45 s, about 2.0 m), so a slower walk steps
  less often.

**What the car asks** (the car repair ADR). The raised car's underside sits at `clearance_m` (its RD2 placeholder,
1.4 m), between the crouched and the standing heights, so only a crouched player gets under it. The car's body is on
a client-only collision layer 4 (its RE8), which the host's world never holds. Its RD1 read-back, "by its height:
the host checks nothing new", is this ADR's KD3; the engineer answered both (a)
([PR #709, comment 6097878738](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game/pull/709#issuecomment-6097878738)).

**What else holds.** A knocked-down player never crouches: today it crawls lying down, and #728's rework (designed,
not built) leaves it unable to move at all. No marker shows through walls (DD4). Bots do not play the House
(ARCHITECTURE §9.7), so no bot needs to crouch.

**Where it plays.** The base mode's maps are the flat greybox (its first, the bots' test map, where every scenario,
the chaos run and the perf run play) and the House (`maps` in `content/modes/base_mode.tres`); its Lobby and Countdown
play on a level of their own (`lobby_level`), its Loading, Round and End on the match's map (`PhaseSpec.level`). The
engineer's answer puts the crouch on **the House only**, and his standing rule keeps every new mechanic off the
greybox: the House-only mechanics are scoped by the mode's data, as the Generator is by `TaskType.maps` (PR #695's
G8) and the knockdown's launch and slide are (PR #754). KE10 does the same for the crouch.

### The numbers (placeholders, "not a decision")
Four new `PlayerRules` numbers, in the base mode's `.tres` with the others, each the engineer's to tune after a
playtest; he took them as proposed (comment 6097878317), still placeholders. The client reads them as it reads
every movement number (`client/CLAUDE.md`), so none goes into `PlayerTuning`.

| `PlayerRules` | Placeholder | Why this value | The mode check's bounds (placeholders) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `crouch_height_m` | 1.2 m | two thirds of the standing 1.8 m; 0.2 m under the car's raised underside (1.4 m, the car repair ADR's RD2), so the car passes with room to spare | from twice `capsule_radius_m` (the least height of a capsule) to `capsule_height_m` |
| `crouch_eye_height_m` | 1.0 m | 0.2 m under the crouched top, as the standing eye is under the standing top | from 0 to `crouch_height_m` |
| `crouch_speed_mps` | 2.0 m/s | under half the walk (4.5 m/s), twice the crawl (1.0 m/s) | from 0.1 to `walk_speed_mps` |
| `crouch_fast_speed_mps` | 2.8 m/s | "a bit faster" with Shift, still under the walk | from `crouch_speed_mps` to `walk_speed_mps` |

The view eases down to the crouched eye and back at the step's catch-up rate (`PlayerTuning.view_catch_up_seconds`,
0.1 s per step height: the 0.6 m in 0.2 s), client feel with no number of its own until a playtest asks for one.
The car's underside and `crouch_height_m` must stay apart by a margin whichever is tuned (R5's check).

KD1's quieter steps (b) add two client numbers beside the footsteps' own (`FootstepCadence`, `SoundChooser`, #525):
client feel, no game rule the host reads, so they are constants there as the cadence's are, which the engineer's
listening session tunes. Both are placeholders, "not a decision".

| Footsteps | Placeholder | Why this value |
|---|---|---|
| The quiet step's volume | 6 dB below a walk's step (`volume_db`, added to the muffle's) | about half as loud; still heard, so a crouch is quieter, not silent |
| The quiet step's hearing range | 6 m (a walk's: 12 m, `SoundChooser.HEARING_RANGE_M`) | half the walk's; `max_distance` and `SoundChooser.step`'s cut both take it |

Where the crouch plays (KE10), a step at or below `crouch_fast_speed_mps` takes both; one at `walk_speed_mps` or
faster takes today's; between them both ease linearly with the speed. Elsewhere every step is today's. The steps'
slower cadence needs no number: the held stride gives it (Context).

**The controller, step by step** (727b):
1. The body wants to crouch while the crouch key is held, the player is living (the knocked-down and the dead never
   crouch) and the crouch plays where it is (KE10: `Game` says so; never in the lobby or on the greybox).
2. Not crouched and wanting: the crouched capsule at once, on the floor or in the air (only the head moves), but not
   in a jump from the crouch before it lands (step 5).
3. Crouched and not wanting: the stand-up test (KE6); with room, the standing capsule; without, it stays (KD4 (a)).
4. Crouched, Shift puts it in the sprint state by the sprint's own rules (`PredictedStamina.can_sprint`: the start
   threshold, the stamina left), in which it moves at `crouch_fast_speed_mps` and pays the sprint's cost; else it
   moves at `crouch_speed_mps` (KD5 (b)). Steps, slopes and the push work as standing.
5. Space while crouched (KD6 (b)): the stand-up test (KE6); with room, the standing capsule and the jump in the same
   physics step, and no crouch again before it lands (on landing, step 2 crouches it again while Ctrl is held);
   without room, no jump and no stand.
6. `ClientSession.set_motion` takes each step's crouch, and a client tick's claim carries its last step's: a pose,
   so no latch.
7. A teleport keeps the crouch; a standing body first takes the crouched capsule at its new transform, then runs the
   stand-up test (KE6) from there and stands again only when it finds room. (`test_move` sweeps the body's current
   shape, so a standing body sweeping its standing capsule upward would probe the space above its head, not whether
   it fits where it landed: a player corrected under the raised car would stay standing inside it.) A life change into
   the living does the same; one out of it restores the standing shape.

**Who sees what** (ARCHITECTURE §5). A crouch is as public as the position: every client draws every living
player's pose. No event, no hidden information. The leak test (`tests/harness/scenario_invariants.gd`) keeps an
allowlist of what anyone may see of another player, `AVATAR_FIELDS`, and fails any snapshot avatar holding another
key; a unit test keeps a second copy, the exact keys of another player's snapshot avatar

public avatar field as `downed` is, and to ARCHITECTURE §5's list of the public avatar fields: the one deliberate
per-peer-filtering step of this design, in three places (`netcode-security-reviewer` reviews it).

## Alternatives
- **A toggle crouch** (KE8 (b)): the engineer decided hold.
- **A slide** (a sprint into a crouch keeps the sprint's speed for a moment): not asked for, and a timing skill
  against the pillar of macro skill over micro skill.
- **A crouch jump** that tucks the legs (the capsule shrinks from the bottom in the air): it reaches ledges a jump
  does not, by timing, and the feet the host checks would rise with no jump (KE4).
- **A crouch as a pose only** (KD3 (b), the car repair ADR's first RD1 recommendation): the engineer chose a crouch
  with a lower body.
- **Lying down or a crawl for the living**: not asked for.
- **Footsteps as a walk's, or silent when drawn crouched** (KD1 (a), (c)): the engineer chose quieter and slower
  steps, (b), read from the speed.
- **A name plate over the crouched head, none while crouched, or one at the standing height** (KD2 (a) to (c)): the
  engineer wants no name plates over any player (#756).
- **A free faster crouch walk** (KD5 (a)): the engineer chose the sprint's cost.
- **A jump that keeps the crouch, or no jump while crouched** (KD6 (a), (c)): the engineer chose to stand up, then
  jump.
- **A crouch only as a look under the car, a host check of the fitter's crouch** (KD3 (b), (c)), **standing up at
  once** (KD4 (b)), **the standing capsule as the target** (KD7 (b)), **actions refused while crouched** (KD8 (b)):
