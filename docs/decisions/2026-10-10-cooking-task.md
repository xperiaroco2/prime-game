## Context
The engineer's rules (#682, "Decided"), in short: Cooking is a task type; one burger (one order) is one subtask, the
host sets the count (default 1). Every order hangs on the kitchen's order board from the round's start and shows a
bun and a patty as pictures and a herb as an icon, which only the greenhouse's herb board decodes, with a mapping new
each round. There are 3 buns, 3 patties (told apart by look, never named after animals) and 5 herbs. Each ingredient
is a one-handed item; the belt holds one. One box per bun and patty kind starts in storage: a two-handed item that
gives its ingredient wherever it stands, one per press of E, without limit, to anyone; a dissident may hide one. The
herb beds give one herb per press. The grill takes one patty at a time: 10 s to fry, then 5 s to take it off before
it burns, black and unusable; a fried patty stays fried. Each order has its own place in the kitchen with a plate
already on it; E with an ingredient puts it on, in any order; each one is checked at once, a wrong one red and taken
back, a right one green and fixed; the burger is done on the spot once its bun, patty and herb are green. #682's
three open items he answered on 2026-10-10 (§5): at most 3 burgers; his words for the buns and the patties, the rest
drafted for him and taken as drafted (§5.1); a herb may go onto a box carrier's belt, which he then widened to every
take beside a two-handed item (§9). His answers add rules of their own: a take with full hands puts the hand item
down at the taker's feet, an ingredient left lying vanishes after a while, the plate refuses a raw or burnt patty,
and the herbs move among the beds each round.

Five things are new to the engine, beyond what the Generator brings:
- **Items made during a round.** Today every item is spawned in the deal (`SpawnItems`, Delivery's packages). A box
  and a bed make a new item on every press.
- **A station that holds an item and a timed station state.** The grill holds one patty and changes it over time,
  with no player channelling; a place holds the ingredients put on it.
- **An item that changes.** A patty goes from raw to fried to burnt and keeps what it became.
- **An item that vanishes.** Today an item lives until `ResetMatch`; an ingredient left lying goes away on its own
  (CD6 (c)).
- **A take beside a two-handed item.** Today a pick-up with a two-handed item in the hand leaves that item where the
  picked one lay (`Items.take`, §7.1.11); the engineer's rule for every take (CD3 (b)) belts a one-handed new item
  instead and keeps the two-handed one in the hands, the floor's pick-up included (CE21).

What already holds (ARCHITECTURE §9, §5; the Generator ADR's Context): tasks are shared and only living players do
subtasks (#79, V4); hidden by sight is a client rule, every item reaching every player through reliable events (the
snapshot holds avatars only, §4.3.5) and the client drawing it only where it lies; items move only through `Items`
(`core/items/items.gd`: `take`, `swap`, `place`, the belt); an item id is a `u16` on the wire (0xFFFF is none, §4.3.1);
the reliable-intent bucket bounds each peer at 100, refilled at 20 a second (§4.5.6).

### 2. The rules as the engine runs them (with the engineer's answers)
1. **The deal** (when `DealTasks` draws Cooking). Stations on their kinds' spawn tags (exact counts, as the
   Generator's GE10, so nothing is drawn): the order board, the herb board and the grill on their one marker each, then
   a bed on each `herb_bed` marker in level order (exactly as many as `herbs`); then N places on the first N `place`
   markers in level order, N the subtasks setting. With N = 0 nothing at all is dealt (no station, no box, no order, no
   `HerbsDealt`), as Delivery's `deal` returns at once: the task has no subtasks and is done (#79), and a done task
   would only scatter boxes nobody needs. Then one box per box kind on a random marker of its tag
   (`cooking_boxes`), each with `ItemSpawned` and `item_rested` (spawn), as Delivery's packages. Then per place, in
   station order, its order: a bun, a raw patty kind and a herb, each drawn independently (`cooking_orders`, CD11).
   Then the code, a random permutation of the herbs over the icons 0 to 4 (`herb_code`), and the beds, a random
   permutation of the herbs over the beds (`herb_beds`, CD13 (b)). Station ids follow that order. A map without the
   markers deals nothing and logs a match error, as Delivery's does (the fit check keeps a match from getting there).
2. **Taking from a box** is `Interact` naming the box, from a living player in Round. An unknown item, or one whose
   kind has no `target_actions`, is refused `nothing_to_do` (CE1). A box's take is refused, in this order,
   `unavailable` (the box is carried), `out_of_reach` (farther than 2 m from the feet of the last accepted claim),
   `blocked` (no line of sight) or `too_soon` (the player took from a box or a bed less than 0.25 s ago: CE17's flood
   guard, not a game rule). Nothing in the hands refuses it (CD3 (b): every take; CD5 (b)). Applied
   (`GiveItem`): at the kind's cap the oldest loose one vanishes first (CE3); a new item of the box's ingredient kind
   is spawned at the box's position (`ItemSpawned`) and given to the taker (`Items.give`, CE19, CE20), which makes
   room as the table below says; `ItemPickedUp` ends it.
3. **Picking from a bed** is `Interact` naming the bed: refused `out_of_reach` (the feet outside the bed's cylinder,
   `AtStation`), `blocked` or `too_soon` (CE17); applied, `Cooking.use_station` gives the herb this round's deal put
   on that bed, as a box gives (the cap, the spawn at the bed's position, `Items.give`).
4. **The grill** is `Interact` naming the grill: refused `out_of_reach` or `blocked`, then by
   `Cooking.use_problem`: at an empty grill, a two-handed hand item gives `two_handed` (a put is not a take, so busy
   hands hold, CD3 (b)), no hand item `empty_hand`, and anything but a raw patty `wrong_item`; an occupied grill
   refuses nothing in the hands, since taking its patty off is a take (CD3 (b)). The rule has no `HandNotTwoHanded`,
   which would refuse the take-off too. Applied: an empty grill takes the hand's raw patty, locked on the grill, its
   fried tick (now plus 200) and burnt tick (now plus 300) in the task state, with `GrillLoaded`; an occupied grill
   gives its patty, whatever its kind, to the player (`Items.give`, which makes room, CD3 (b), CD5 (b)), and is
   empty. Every tick of
   a phase that lists `TaskTicks` (Round): a patty on the grill whose fried tick has come becomes its fried kind, one
   whose burnt tick has come becomes the burnt kind (`ItemChanged`).
5. **A place** is `Interact` naming a place: refused `two_handed`, `out_of_reach` or `blocked`, then by
   `Cooking.use_problem`: `unavailable` (the burger is done), `empty_hand`, or `wrong_item` (an item that is none of
   the task's ingredient kinds, such as a knife, or a raw or burnt patty, CD8 (b)). Applied: the hand item goes onto
   the place. It is **right** when its kind is the order's bun, fried patty or herb and that slot is still empty:
   locked there, its slot filled. Otherwise it is **wrong** (another bun, fried patty or herb, or a second one for a
   filled slot): it lies on the ground at the place, interactive, so `PickUp` takes it back. `IngredientPlaced` either
   way. The third right one: the place is done, then `Tasks.subtask_done` (detail: the order's index).
6. **Vanishing** (CD6 (c), C10). Every tick of a phase that lists `VanishTicks` (Round), an item of a kind with
   `vanish_seconds` that has lain on the ground that long since it last came to rest is removed, with `ItemVanished` (in ascending item id when several go in one tick);
   picked up and put down again, it starts over. Every move that leaves an item lying sets its rest tick, through one
   `Items` helper that alone writes the ground state: a put-down or a drop, a throw's landing, the deal's spawn, a wrong ingredient laid
   at a place (`on_station`), the put-down a take makes room with; a move into a hand, onto a belt or into the locked
   state clears it. So a bun carried for minutes and laid red on a plate lies its full 60 s there. A locked item (on the grill, green on a plate) does not lie, so it
   never vanishes; the boxes, the knives and the packages have no `vanish_seconds`.
7. **Nothing else** changes the cooking. A hit, a knockdown, a death or a leave drops what the player carries, as for
   any item (ARCHITECTURE §9.3, Life): a carried box drops at the body and gives there; a dropped ingredient lies and
   vanishes in time. The grill goes on frying: the patty belongs to no player. `TaskTicks` and `VanishTicks` run only
   in Round; `ResetMatch` clears the items, stations and task state.

How `Items.give` makes room (CD3 (b), CD5 (b), both the engineer's; one move for a box's take, a bed's and the
grill's take-off; the last two rows are his belt rule for every take beside a two-handed item, which a pick-up from
the floor follows too, CE21). A given kind is always one-handed (CE14), so the belt can always take it:

| The hands before | The belt | Where the new item goes | Events before `ItemPickedUp` |
|---|---|---|---|
| empty | any | the hand | none |
| a one-handed item | empty | the hand; the hand item to the belt (`belted`, as a pick-up today) | none |
| a one-handed item | full | the hand; the hand item put down at the taker's feet (CD5 (b), CE19) | `ItemPlaced` (`put_down`) |
| a two-handed item (a box, a package) | empty | the belt (CD3 (b)): `ItemPickedUp` with `belted` the item itself (CE20); the two-handed item stays in the hand | none |
| a two-handed item | full | the belt: the belt's item put down at the taker's feet first (CD3 (b), CE19), then the new item onto the belt (`belted` the item itself, CE20); the two-handed item stays in the hand | `ItemPlaced` (`put_down`) |

A pick-up from the floor (`PickUp`) with a two-handed item in the hand follows the last two rows when the picked item
is one-handed (a red ingredient taken back, a dropped herb, a knife): the same events, through the same move (CE21).
A two-handed picked item (a box picked up by holding E, a package) keeps today's swap, the carried one resting where
the picked one lay: the belt holds only a one-handed item, so the belt rule cannot apply. With a one-handed hand
item a pick-up stays as today (§7.1.11).

### 9. Needs the engineer
Only his approval of this design in its PR. His answers of 2026-10-10 settle every question: CD1 to CD17 (§5) and the
four those left, each decided by him (chat with the game-design manager session, 09:10 to 09:35 UTC):
1. **The drafts of §5.1** (CD2): the task's name "Cooking", the label "Burgers (Cooking)", the item names, the icons
   (a sun, a moon, a star, a drop, a heart) and the looks: **as drafted**
   ([comment 6096108400](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game/pull/701#issuecomment-6096108400), item 1).
2. **The herbs** (CD2): basil, dill, rosemary, chives and mint, **as drafted** (the same comment, item 2).
3. **How far CD3 (b) reaches: every take** ([comment 6096157421](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game/pull/701#issuecomment-6096157421),
   which closes the read-back of [comment 6096140448](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game/pull/701#issuecomment-6096140448);
   together they replace item 3 of 6096108400, his first word, under which a two-handed item would have refused every
   interaction). Every take works while the hands hold a two-handed item (a box, a package): a take from a box, a herb
   from a bed, the grill's patty, a pick-up of a loose item from the floor. The new item goes onto the belt; a full
   belt's item drops at the taker's feet; the two-handed item stays in the hands (§2's table, CE19 to CE21). His
   reason: "so as not to wear the player out with needless steps": the rule does for the player the routine of
   putting the box down, swapping and picking it up again. What is not a take (putting onto a plate or the grill,
   using a station that gives no item, a hit; a box's, a bed's and the grill's take-off are takes) keeps the
   busy-hands refusal (`two_handed`). This replaces the derived points of the first draft: (i) a take from another
   box onto the belt stands, now his; (ii) the box put down at the feet for a take with a full belt is gone; (iii)
   the plates and a put on the grill still refuse a box carrier, while a take off the grill no longer does.
4. **Where a displaced hand item lands** (CD5 (b)): **at the taker's feet** (the same comment 6096108400, item 4,
   the question's (a)), CE19's spot, no wire change.

The placeholder numbers (his CD6 N, 60 s here, and CD16's 2 m; the manager's 0.25 s of CE17 and cap of 128 of CE3)
are tuned in the playtest; none blocks an issue.

## Alternatives
- **A new intent for boxes** (CE1 (b)) or **`PickUp` with a flag** (CE1 (c)): one intent per mechanic, or one verb
  meaning two things for one item kind.
- **A box as a moving station** (CE1 (d)): stations stand where the deal put them.
- **A new event for a given item** (CE2 (b)): `ItemSpawned` and `ItemPickedUp` already say it.
- **The oldest loose item to the taker at a cap of 32** (CE3 (b), the first design's CD6 (a)): the engineer chose
  vanishing, and 32 a kind stops the spam he allows.
- **No cap** (CE3 (c)): vanishing and the flood guard bound the items only as long as N stays short.
- **No id reuse**: a 60-minute match of spamming players runs out of `u16` ids, since a vanish frees none by itself.
- **A refusal for full hands** (`hands_full`, the first design's CD5 (a)): the engineer chose the put-down.
- **`Interact` with a facing** (CE19 (b)): a wire field on every station use for where one displaced item lands.
- **A new event for an item straight onto the belt** (CE20 (b)): `ItemPickedUp`'s `belted` already names it.
- **A two-handed item refusing every interaction** (the engineer's first word on CD3's reach, comment 6096108400's
  item 3, which he replaced once the belt's rows were explained, comments 6096140448 and 6096157421): a box carrier
  would put the box down for every herb.
- **The carried box put down for a take with a full belt** (the first draft's derived point (ii)): the engineer
  chose the belt swap, the box staying in the hands.
- **The floor's pick-up left as today** (CE21 (d)) or **with a belt branch of its own** (CE21 (b)): the routine his
  rule removes, or two copies of one rule.
- **A cooking stage per item** (CE4 (b), (c)): state beside the kind, read by the HUD, the check and the look.
- **One ingredient item kind with a variant field**: fewer files, but a new item field on the wire and in the HUD,
  where 21 kinds reuse `ItemSpawned`'s `kind` and the display name as they are.
- **Every item on a station on the ground** (CE5 (b)): `PickUp` would take a green ingredient back.
- **One station kind per herb on fixed beds** (CE6 (c), this ADR's first design): the herbs could not move among
  the beds each round (CD13 (b)).
- **One station kind per herb on a shared bed tag** (CE6 (b)): five kinds on one tag, against ZE3's check.
- **Boxes in the base mode's deal** (CE7 (b)): boxes in matches without Cooking.
- **The orders' herbs on the wire** (CE9 (b)): an honest client one slip from showing the code at the kitchen.
- **The beds' herbs in an event of their own** (CE9 (d)): a second row for a list the same deal draws.
- **`ZoneProgress` for the grill** (CE10 (b)): one threshold where the grill has two.
- **The bucket alone for takes** (CE17 (c)): up to 80 reliable events a second from one spamming peer to everyone.
- **Placeholder tags for the given-only kinds** (CE18 (b)): 15 tags that name no marker.
