# Brainstorm 1, 2026-10-15

Raw ideas from the design session's chat, for the engineer to review in a batch. **Ideas, not decisions:** nothing
here is a rule until the engineer picks it and it is recorded in `../m8-match.md` or `../../GDD.md`. Numbers are
placeholders. Marks: **liked** = the engineer said so in the chat; **engineer's** = his own idea.

## Harder tasks: find, look up, remember
1. **The party playlist.** A guest asks for "that song"; find the record at the player by its cover. Covert spoiling:
   put the wrong one on, which annoys the guests.
2. **The recipe on the fridge.** The house cocktail's recipe is pinned on the kitchen fridge, the bar is in the
   garden: remember three ingredients and carry them over. A human may "mix one up".
3. **The Wi-Fi password.** The guests want internet; the password is on the router in the attic, typed in
   downstairs. One player runs and dictates by voice: communication as a mechanic.
4. **The seating plan.** Who sits with whom hangs in one room, the chairs stand in another; a wrong plan makes the
   guests quarrel loudly enough for the neighbours to hear.
5. **Which alien vegetable.** The garden's vegetables look alike; the right one is only pictured in the recipe book.
   The wrong one spoils the burger.
6. **Find the key.** The garage key is hidden somewhere in the House, in a random place each match. Whoever finds it
   may not hand it over.
7. **The safe's combination.** The digits are spread over the House (a calendar, a clock, a photo); put them together
   by voice.

## Mischief the neighbours notice
8. **A lawnmower at 3 a.m.** Start it and let it drive itself.
9. **The inflatable.** Blow up a giant inflatable alien over the fence; it blocks the neighbours' view.
10. **Barbecue smoke into the neighbours' window.** Turn the fan round.
11. **The neighbours' dog.** Open the gate and let it out; ongoing: it barks until someone brings it back.
12. **Karaoke for the whole street.** Turn the microphone on and **your own voice** comes out of the speakers for
    everyone, the neighbours included: mischief made of the player's voice.

## The humans' tools against the aliens' talk
13. **A jammer.** Noise near a station shrinks everyone's voice range around it.
14. **Radios with static.** If the aliens get radios, a human can break them: hiss instead of voice.
15. **A false alarm.** Plant "rubbish" near the phone while the real problem is elsewhere: the aliens run the wrong
    way.
16. **A megaphone,** bought with points: your voice carries over the whole House, to steer the chaos or to announce a
    lie.

## Gadgets and mutations (M8.3)
17. **Radar ears.** Hear voices through walls for 10 s; visible on the body (the ears glow).
18. **Plasticine camouflage.** Pose as furniture or a vase for 20 s.
19. **Long arms.** Take items across a table.
20. **A loud laugh.** Other players' laughter gives you stamina back: a social mutation.

## Party events: randomness mid-match
21. **The mother-in-law arrives.** An NPC guest to please within a minute, or the anger rises.
22. **A blackout in the street.** Dark for everyone; only phones give light.
23. **The pizza goes to the wrong address.** Whoever intercepts it first earns a point.
24. **A meteor shower.** The aliens greet "their own"; everyone goes out into the garden: chaos.

## Funny moments around death
25. **A faulty capsule.** Rarely you respawn with an odd mutation (a huge head, a chipmunk voice) for 30 s: a random
    treat, not a punishment.
26. **Pay respects in five seconds** (**liked**). At a body you can "pay respects": it does nothing, it makes a clip.
