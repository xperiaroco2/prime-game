# Ideas batch 2, 2026-10-15 (the map: from the House to the estate)

Ideas from the design session's chat in the evening of 2026-10-15, for the engineer to review in a batch. **Ideas,
not decisions**: a decided rule lives in `../m8-match.md`, `../../GDD.md` or the issue named. **Engineer's** marks his
own idea; the rest are the design session's or the research's proposals. Numbers are placeholders.

## The map's direction (recorded on #1035)
- **Engineer's:** the map is the foundation; nothing else makes sense until it is really good. Do not build it around
  the neighbours, the shop or the cameras; the anger meter may become an abstract suspicion meter.
- **Engineer's:** a mesh, not a hub with spokes: every place two or more ways in, links between places, shortcuts that
  can be unlocked, pleasantly confusing ("I still checked the map after 50 matches").
- **Engineer's:** every room has a reason (in Lockdown Protocol every room has something); rebuild rather than patch.
- **Engineer's:** a map that can grow: built for today's mechanics, with spots kept for new ones (another floor,
  another building, another passage).
- **Engineer's:** a dacha first (separate small buildings, three levels), then **an estate** instead: an alien lord's
  manor on its own grounds bounded by tall hedges, the elite "prime" party; the glass orangery as a botanical garden
  of alien plants. Terraces liked ("fun to run around"), but not too open and not too closed: hedges and walls that make
  you go round through another place.
- Design session: method in pairs (no place without a mechanic, no mechanic without a place); three materials for
  voice (hedges hide you but pass voice, glass shows but muffles, masonry and the servants' door block both).

## The estate's places (engineer's unless noted)
- **Library:** a cipher (below) and a bookcase door.
- **A dark room, a pool,** secret passages; **servants' corridors** behind the walls (design session, from research).
- **Portraits of lords as aliens,** with a monocle and lordly moustaches; a normal manor with alien touches everywhere.
- **Secrets found in play:** a newcomer not knowing is fine; found in about 5 games or shown by a friend; "did you
  know you can do this here?". Design session: known places with tonight's active one drawn per match; an opened
  secret stays open; secrets never decide the match.

## Tasks (recorded on #1033)
- **The library cipher** (engineer's, refined with the design session): a lock shows glyphs of one alien script, a
  codebook in another room maps it to a second alien script, new each match; players describe the glyphs by voice
  ("like an S with two little circles, a snake"); voice-first; a silent player can do it alone by running and
  remembering.
- **A safe mini-game** (engineer's): crack the dial; inside a revolver with 6 rounds, gone when fired. Research warns:
  a weapon behind a lock causes a rush at the start.
- **The pool** (engineer's): find a net, skim the leaves, change the water.
- **Cocktails** like the burgers (engineer's).
- **An orangery task** like an analysis of plants, not copied; every plant alien (engineer's).
- **All food alien** (engineer's): alien crabs, herbs, meat, buns; maybe not burgers at all later.
- **The burger is checked when served** (engineer's, #1101): carried to its order; only "wrong" is shown; the cook
  works out which part by eye; the herb is the cheatable part; a wrong burger is redone whole.
- **The photo as a pose silhouette** (engineer's, #1102): the subject must match the silhouette for the shot to work;
  no printing and hanging.
- **The car repair as the ship repair** (engineer's): a wrong part explodes, as Lockdown Protocol's pressure task.
- **A loudspeaker in the cameras room** (engineer's): talk to everyone, or play music over the whole location, which
  annoys everyone.

## Voice (recorded on #1033)
- **Engineer's rule:** voice-first; every task can still be done alone, slower; do not simplify a mechanic for edge
  cases; a silent player chooses the voice-free tasks (deliveries).

## Respawn (recorded on #1032)
- **Engineer's:** no capsule props; marked respawn points; a small drone flies in and "3D-prints" you back.
- Research: about 4.5 s on top of the 30 s wait (a short telegraph, a 1.5 s print with one comic flaw, a "fresh print"
  sheen during the invulnerability); points disguised as manor fixtures (a clock, a wardrobe, a fountain, a portrait).
