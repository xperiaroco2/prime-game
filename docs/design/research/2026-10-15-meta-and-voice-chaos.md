# Meta-progression without power creep, and the proximity-voice "chaos co-op" wave: research report

Date of research: 2026-10-15. Labels used throughout: **[V]** verified from a cited source, **[P]** player or press opinion, **[A]** my analysis. Where a source was a search-result summary and I did not read the page, I say so. Sales figures are estimates unless a developer stated them. Several tracker sites contradict each other, and I flag that where it matters.

---

## PART A. Meta-progression without power creep

### A1. The core tension
Meta-progression keeps people returning, but anything that makes veterans stronger punishes newcomers. The games below solve this in five ways: cosmetics only, difficulty gating, sidegrades, resets, and match-scoped power. Each one has a different cost for a hobby team.

### A2. Cosmetics only: Among Us, Fall Guys, Valorant, Rocket League
**Among Us** [V, via search summary, not fully fetched]
- The free currency (Beans) buys "Cosmicubes". Playing earns Pods that walk a branching unlock path. XP from playtime multiplies Bean and Pod gain. Stars are the paid currency. Innersloth stated all cosmetics are purely visual ([EGM](https://egmnow.com/among-us-update-adds-roles-cosmicubes-and-more/), [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/what-beans-among-us)).
- Sensor Tower estimated $39M from cosmetics on a $5 PC / free mobile game ([Android Central](https://androidcentral.com/among-us-has-surpassed-over-217-million-installs-android-and-ios)). That is a third-party estimate.
- I found no source tying a cosmetic event to measurable player return. The Udonis monthly-user rebound (about 17.2M in Oct 2024 to over 20M in Feb 2025) began before the Feb 2025 event, so the link is unproven ([Udonis](https://blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/among-us-player-count)). That is a marketing firm's estimate, not official data.

**Fall Guys** [V]
- The June 2022 free-to-play relaunch split currencies. Show-Bucks are premium. Kudos are no longer awarded for finishing matches. Crowns became a rank that unlocks costumes. Free Season Pass players get 50 of 100 tiers ([official post](https://www.fallguys.com/fr/news/upcoming-fall-guys-currency-changes?lang=en-US)).
- [P] Reaction was negative ([TechRaptor](https://Techraptor.net/gaming/news/fall-guys-free-to-play-currency-system-angers-fans)). 20M players arrived within 48 hours of going free ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2022/06/fall-guys-has-20-million-players-after-free-to-play-release)). Steam trackers show a long decline, but they disagree and cover Steam only, so I do not rely on them.
- [A] Lesson: removing a reward players already earned by playing (Kudos per match) angered people even though power was untouched. Never take away an earn-by-playing source.

**Valorant and Rocket League** [V]
- Riot's rule is that cosmetics must never give an advantage ([PCInvasion citing Riot](https://www.pcinvasion.com/?p=217890)). Riot also promised no loot boxes ([Inven](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/10584/valorants-in-game-rewards-will-include-sale-of-cosmetics-but-will-not-include-loot-boxes)).
- Psyonix stated a "Don't Sell Advantage" policy in 2017 ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/rocket-league-is-getting-unlockable-crates)).
- Neither statement links the policy to retention. That link is my inference.
- [P] Players still perceive skins as an edge ([Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/valorant/news/are-valorant-skins-pay-to-win-a-look-into-the-cosmetic-side-of-riots-tactical-shooter)). Even cosmetics need to be readable and not hide information.

### A3. Cosmetics driving a comedic co-op game's return: R.E.P.O.
- [V] Update 0.4.0 "The Cosmetic Update" landed 2026-05-07. It added over 500 cosmetic items, a redesigned shop, a new mode and new items ([ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/steam-most-popular-horror-game-2025-is-back-at-the-top-of-the-charts-thanks-to-a-major-update/), [iXBT](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/05/10/onlain-indi-khorrora-repo-uvelichilsia-v-neskolko-raz-posle-reliza-kosmeticheskogo-obnovleniia.html)). The daily peak rose from about 35k to over 96.9k. Raijin shows May 2026 average daily players of 50,293 (+117.7%), then a decline to 15,640 in October ([Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/3241660/REPO)).
- [A] This is correlation. The update also had a new mode, new items, and a 30% discount that ended May 11 (ComicBook). Neither source says how the cosmetics are earned, so I cannot say whether they are grindable.
- [V] Earlier spikes followed other updates too. mein-mmo notes the last 115k level followed an October 2025 update ([mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/hit-steam-96-prozent-positiv-so-viele-spieler-wie-lange-nicht-update/)). The pattern is spike and decay, which fits a content-drop return model more than a steady-retention model.
- R.E.P.O. already had permanent per-run upgrades (health, stamina, strength) bought with run currency before the cosmetics ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R.E.P.O.), upgrade prices from guides vary).

### A4. Difficulty gating instead of power: PEAK
- [V] PEAK has 10 ascent levels (2 default, 8 unlockable). Completing one unlocks the next. Higher levels stack modifiers such as double fall damage, faster hunger, heavier items and colder nights. Rewards are cosmetic sashes. Only one player in a group needs the ascent unlocked, and all players get the rewards ([PEAK wiki](https://peak.wiki.gg/Ascents)). Badges give cosmetic rewards, and updates added 10 and 11 new badges ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/all-peak-badges-and-how-to-get-them/)).
- [V] The map changes every 24 hours, at 17:00 UTC ([HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/lang/ka/inside-peaks-daily-map-generation-system) says 14 pre-made daily map files, picked daily).
- Why it works [A]: veterans chase harder modifiers, not stronger characters, so a newcomer joining a veteran's lobby is not outclassed. The "one unlock lifts the whole group" rule removes the social friction of a gated friend.
- What goes wrong: a Chinese-language source suggests the team's aim was a finished product, not progression. I found no developer statement on why PEAK has no upgrades. A single guide claims a full run takes about five hours, and group versus solo is unclear ([Deltias](https://deltiasgaming.com/?p=277975)).

### A5. Sidegrades and shared-pool variety
**Deep Rock Galactic** [V]
- Promotion needs class level 25. It resets that class to level 1 and keeps unlocks. The first promotion gives a perk slot. Later promotions give a Blank core, an Overclock-Infused core, and a Cosmetic-Infused core. Costs rise linearly (credits plus class-specific minerals) ([wiki](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Promotions)).
- The wiki lists 160 overclocks in three classes (Clean, Balanced, Unstable), and the specific overclock a core yields is random on receipt ([wiki](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Overclocks)). The wiki page was truncated at 100,000 of 219,000 characters, so counts per class are partial.
- [P] Steam threads on the Survivor spin-off complain that many overclocks are barely distinguishable from normal upgrades, that some options are strictly worse, and that unlock gating is a grind ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2321470/discussions/0/6292161380806029016)). I found no Ghost Ship statement framing overclocks as sidegrades. That framing is mine.
- [A] The retention hook is variety: collecting options and trying combinations. The risks are overlap (two options doing the same job) and "trap" options.

**Splatoon 3** [V]
- Gear has a main ability and up to three random sub-slots. Ability chunks let players choose: 10 to add a sub-ability, 45 to replace a main ([Nintendo](https://splatoon.nintendo.com/en/news/squid-research-lab-dives-deep-into-the-splatlands), [Splatoon wiki](https://splatoonwiki.org/wiki/Ability_chunk)). I did not find the cost table on a primary page.
- [P] Fan tier lists still rank some abilities far above others ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Splatoon-3/archives/388867)). One progression essay praises Splatoon's mix of cosmetic and mild stat gear ([Vivien Moll](https://vivienmoll.substack.com/p/making-better-progression-systems)). I found no Nintendo statement on the design intent.
- [A] "Mild" stat gear still creates a meta. Small number sidegrades are not automatically safe.

**Phasmophobia** [V]
- The Ascension update (v0.9, Aug 2023) added prestige. New players start at Prestige 0; existing players were moved to Prestige 1. Each equipment item gained 3 tiers (22 items became 60), and tier costs are paid in money but not in contract load. XP converts 1:1 to money ([Kinetic Games](https://kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia-ascension)).
- Kinetic Games reported over 20M copies across platforms in Oct 2024 ([press release](https://gamedeveloper.com/press-release/over-20-million-sold-across-all-platforms)).
- [A] Tiered gear is power, but its price is spent between contracts. The open question is whether higher tiers make cooperation less necessary.

**Hunt: Showdown** [V]
- Patch 1.96 (Dec 2023) unlocked all base weapons at Rank 1. Variants and ammo unlock with XP, and every gameplay item is unlocked by Bloodline Rank 64. The stated goal is that "no unlock is ever more than two kills away" ([mp1st](https://mp1st.com/?p=246854)).
- Prestige resets most progress for small rewards (starting cash or a temporary XP boost, a few skins) ([Prima](https://primagames.com/tips/how-does-prestige-work-in-hunt-showdown)).
- [A] A mature game moved toward unlocking power faster, not slower. Gating power hurt acquisition of new players more than it helped retention.

### A6. Power and perks, with the grind complaints: Dead by Daylight, Helldivers 2
**Dead by Daylight** [V unless noted]
- Level cap is 50 and prestige cap is 100. Prestige costs 20,000 Bloodpoints. Prestiges 1 to 3 unlock Tier I to III of that character's unique perks for all characters, and prestiges 4 to 9 give cosmetics and charms ([wiki](https://deadbydaylight.wiki.gg/wiki/Levels_&_Prestige)).
- The June 2022 update claimed to cut grind by roughly 75% ([EIP](https://eip.gg/dbd/news/dead-by-daylight-developer-update-to-introduces-sweeping-changes-june-2022/)). [P] The grind is still called a long-standing newcomer deterrent, and anniversary events give up to 525% more Bloodpoints ([mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/dead-by-daylight-coddles-its-players-this-reveals-problems,698421), search summary). A September 2026 report says 58 perks are being reworked, with 23 buffed so they are appealing to new players ([PlayNews](https://www.playnews.gg/en/guides/dead-by-daylight-58-perks-reworked-ptb-10-2-0-opens-tuesday-september-15-2026), search summary only, page returned 403).
- [A] A perk pool that is large and permanent creates a veteran advantage that the developers keep paying to repair.

**Helldivers 2** [P/V mixed]
- The CEO said balancing through damage numbers was a blunt tool and that the team had "gone too far in some areas" (2024). Players complained about a free versus paid warbond imbalance. Arrowhead said it would add more freely unlockable vehicles ([mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/helldivers-2-chef-spricht-ueber-nerfs,1124095), search summary). I found no Arrowhead statement on sidegrades as policy.

### A7. Match-scoped power: Overwatch hero perks and Stadium
- [V] Perks arrive during the match. A minor perk is chosen at level 2 and a major perk at level 3, each from two options. Progress comes from damage, eliminations and healing, and accelerates over time. Designers aimed to keep core gameplay intact and iterated through three versions ([Xbox Wire deep dive](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/02/12/deep-dive-overwatch-2-new-perks-system/)).
- [V] Stadium is best-of-7 with Stadium Cash (3,500 start), free Powers at rounds 1/3/5/7 that cannot be removed, up to six items that can be sold for full value, and bounties on threatening opponents when a team is behind ([Blizzard](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24188046/), [Keys to Victory](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24191424/)). On 2025-10-30 Blizzard reverted ranked Stadium to best-of-7, citing feedback and data, and strengthened comeback mechanics ([patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2025/10/), search summary).
- [P] The director acknowledged some builds "went too far" ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/overwatch-2/stadium-overpowered), search summary). Reports say Stadium will get no new heroes or maps, and one opinion piece claims about 6% player share (single source, unverified).
- [A] Match-scoped power resets every match, so nobody carries an edge. The cost is balance work: economy, snowballing and catch-up needed repeated tuning at a large studio.

### A8. Daily and weekly challenge layers
- Phasmophobia: weekly challenge with fixed loadouts, and rewards raised in v0.9 ([Kinetic Games](https://kineticgames.co.uk/news/phasmophobia-ascension)). DRG: Weekly Core Hunt and weekly Deep Dives yield cores ([wiki](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Overclocks)). PEAK: daily map (above). Fall Guys: Daily Challenges award Kudos, Weekly Challenges award Fame.
- [A] These create a reason to return on a specific day, and a shared topic ("what did you get on today's map") without raising power. I found no published retention numbers for any of them.

### A9. What data exists
- I found no public GDC talk or dataset comparing cosmetic and power progression on churn. An essay author says none exists for progression systems generally ([Vivien Moll](https://vivienmoll.substack.com/p/making-better-progression-systems)).
- The best available evidence is update-driven spikes in player counts: R.E.P.O. (above) and Lethal Company, which reportedly hit about 19k concurrent after its v80 update in April 2026 and has since tapered ([Explosion tracker](https://www.explosion.com/game-guides/lethal-company-player-count/), search summary). I treat both as correlation.

### B2. Why they spread
1. **Creators saw it first.** [V] Lethal Company's developer built an audience with earlier games (It Steals, Dead Seater) and posted streamer-reaction compilations before launch. Weekly Monday peaks rose from about 611 (Oct 23, 2023) to about 43k (Nov 13) ([Push to Talk](https://www.pushtotalk.gg/p/how-lethal-company-sold-10-million-copies)). The developer called it "actually a game about laughing at death".
2. **Short video before the game was finished.** [V] Yapyap had a July 2025 TikTok with 288k likes before gameplay was defined, ranked #3 in Valve's October 2025 Next Fest uniques, and players found it mainly via word of mouth and short-form video. Only 12% of surveyed fans were 35 or older ([GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-yapyap-jammed-its-way-to-1-million)).
3. **Price at impulse level.** [V/A] $5.99 to $10 (table). PEAK's cheap price is credited by one outlet ([Outlook](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/from-game-jam-to-10-million-units-indie-hit-peak-success)). Content Warning gave away its first day.
4. **Small, fast teams.** [V] PEAK was built in about four months after a game jam (same Outlook piece). Yapyap's team chose a 3 to 6 month scope after six years on a previous game. Meccha Chameleon is credited to one named developer plus one programmer in some sources and one developer in others, so team size is unverified.
5. **Co-op sells itself.** [P] Destructoid: a co-op game spreads by recommending it to friends ([Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/when-did-lethal-company-come-out-and-how-did-it-get-so-popular/)). The genre is now called "friendslop": cheap, unpolished-looking, one to six players, voice chat, built for streaming ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/best-friendslop-games-you-need-to-play/)).
6. **Mod and map content.** [V] Meccha Chameleon's spread leaned on Steam Workshop maps. The cost: user maps carried malware and the official Discord was hacked (Outlook). [A] User content is a growth lever and an operations risk.

### B3. What a match feels like
Session lengths are mostly not documented in the sources I could read; where I say so, I mean no source gave minutes.
- **Lethal Company** [V/P]: ship, moon, loot, monster, return. Quota starts around 130 and rises 50 to 99% per cycle, over a short deadline ([Switchblade guide](https://www.switchbladegaming.com/co-op-games/lethal-company-quota-guide/), search summary). Failing ends the run. Proximity voice is distance-limited, with walkie-talkies to extend it (Push to Talk). [P] "Every run starts calm and ends in panic" (editorial summarized in a search result; not fetched).
- **R.E.P.O.** [V]: randomly generated levels, quotas per extraction point (one active at a time), physics-based valuables that lose value when damaged, a shop between levels for permanent upgrades ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R.E.P.O.), guides). Session minutes: not documented in sources I read.
- **Content Warning** [V via search summary]: three days to reach a view quota, one dive per day, a team screening of the footage afterward, views convert to money, failing wipes equipment and money. Deaths captured on camera earn views. Source: search result aggregating [ggrecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/guides/what-is-content-warning-explained/) and [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/content-warning-game-guide-and-how-to-get-more-views/); I did not read those pages.
- **PEAK** [V/P]: campfire-separated biomes, a new map daily, one guide gives 30 to 40 minutes per biome and about five hours for a full run (single source, unclear whether solo).
- **Yapyap** [V]: three nights per round with a destruction quota; spells are voiced into the microphone ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yapyap)). Developer says many spells are "funny but shallow".
- **Meccha Chameleon** [V]: seekers and hiders; hiders paint a white character to blend in; host sets round length; public servers are messy, so friends are recommended (GamingOnLinux).
- **Lockdown Protocol** [V/P]: social deduction with tasks and a small share of dissidents; all players have the same abilities ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/is-worth-playing-lockdown-protocol-2025)). The store page lists a "Points Shop" with 10 items and no other described progression. [P] Steam thread (Sep 2024): the same few players are picked as dissidents lobby after lobby ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2780980/discussions/0/4764333414208635266)).

### B4. How funny emergent moments arise
- **Voice range makes a story.** [V] Distance-limited voice, plus walkie-talkies, means splitting up costs you warnings and creates sudden quiet ([Push to Talk](https://www.pushtotalk.gg/p/how-lethal-company-sold-10-million-copies)). [P] A death can cut you off from comms ([Prima Games](https://primagames.com/?p=210331), search summary).
- **Physics plus a goal.** [P] In R.E.P.O. the comedy comes from fumbling a vase when a monster turns the corner (search summary). [A] A clear objective that physics can sabotage makes everybody's mistakes the content.
- **Voice as input.** Yapyap's spells need shouting or silence (search summary).
- **Dying is part of the joke.** Content Warning pays views for filmed deaths (above). [A] That fits our pillar that death is not a loss.
- **Intentional roughness.** [P] One analysis argues the retro jank is deliberate comedy ([search summary](https://www.summerengine.com/blog/games-like-lethal-company)); that is an opinion, not the developer's statement.

### B5. What makes friends come back
- **No published retention study.** The best signals are:
  - **Updates reignite:** R.E.P.O. spikes (A3); Lethal Company's v80 (A9). Lethal Company's earlier v70 was June 2025, so content was sparse in between ([Destructoid via search](https://www.newsbreak.com/destructoid-313390785/4567286767440-lethal-company-v80-full-patch-notes-listed)).
  - **Audience share decays fast.** [V] Chris Zukowski estimates launch hits hold over 50% of the friendslop audience, falling to about 10% within three to nine months. The audience itself grew, peaking over 280k (Mar 2025) and reaching about 366k after Meccha Chameleon (Jun 2026). Players switch between titles ([Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/08/03/analyst-there-are-many-games-in-the-friendslap-niche-but-it-is-not-oversaturated)). Mage Arena held only 11.75% share at peak yet sold 624k copies and earned $3.69M in its first week (same article).
  - **Fresh shared content:** PEAK's daily map and growing badge sets (A4).
  - **Fixed-friend-group nights.** [A] Games that need four or more friends compete on whether the group can schedule, not on depth.
- [A] The wave monetizes one big acquisition spike plus cheap price, and treats retention as repeated visits rather than months of daily play.

## Mapping to our game

[A] Our differences matter:
- We are hidden-role, so progression must not leak role. Anything visible (cosmetics, mutations) must be role-neutral.
- Roles are mechanically identical, so progression cannot be role-specific.
- Our pillar "open knowledge" favours visible, announced mutation pools over hidden ones.
- Our pillar "macro over micro skill" means a mutation like speed or stamina is acceptable only if it does not create an aim or twitch edge.
- Respawn means death is not the cost of an error, so mutations tied to "not dying" matter less.
- The team is one engineer with agents, so each content item (cosmetic, mutation, challenge) has a recurring cost. Data-driven mechanics are cheap per entry but each needs balance review, and the content area is the engineer's.

## 1. Cross-game patterns
1. **Power resets per match, collection persists.** Match-scoped power (Overwatch Stadium, R.E.P.O. run upgrades, Content Warning gear) avoids veteran advantage. What persists is cosmetics, difficulty ladders, or option pools.
2. **Difficulty and variety beat strength.** PEAK ascents, DRG overclocks, Phasmophobia weekly loadouts give veterans something to chase without changing newcomers' odds.
3. **The best-attested return trigger is a content drop**, not a progression bar (R.E.P.O., Lethal Company). Correlation, not proof.
4. **Power systems need ongoing repair.** DbD reworks its grind, Hunt unlocked base weapons at Rank 1, Helldivers rebalanced and added free unlocks, Stadium re-tuned its economy.
5. **Removing earned rewards is punished** (Fall Guys Kudos).
6. **Sidegrades still form a meta** (Splatoon tier lists, DRG overlap complaints).
7. **The wave's engine is moments, price and creators**, with 3 to 6 month scopes and cheap prices.
8. **Social-deduction cousins** have a role-fairness problem (Lockdown Protocol's repeated dissident picks).

## 2. Risks for our design
- **Role leak through cosmetics or mutations.** A mutation or face part that correlates with human or alien breaks the identical-roles premise. Mitigate by making every pool role-neutral.
- **Points-to-mutations feedback loop.** Our confirmed answers give points for tasks and for sabotage. If points buy mutations, a human who farms sabotage points becomes stronger, and an outed human has fewer points (nobody works with him), so the loop can snowball in either direction. Stadium needed bounties and a mercy rule for the same reason.
- **A strong mutation (seeing through walls) dominates** and collapses deduction or sabotage. Open knowledge helps, since everyone can plan for it, but it still needs a counter.
- **Sidegrade overlap and traps** (DRG).
- **Content treadmill.** Cosmetic and mutation pools are recurring work. R.E.P.O. needed over 500 items for its spike.
- **User-content abuse** if we ever allow shared faces or maps (Meccha Chameleon malware).
- **Face sculpting.** A tool that allows offensive shapes needs moderation, and an asymmetric look could reveal a role. I found no source on sculpt-moderation practice, so this is open.
- **Retention expectation.** Our wave peers lose most of the audience within months; a hobby project should plan for that, not fight it.
- **Role assignment fairness** (Lockdown thread) interacts with any meta unlock tied to a role.

## 3. Options for the engineer (not decisions)
All example values are placeholders.

**Option 1: Cosmetic-and-face-only persistent progression.**
- Persistent rewards are purely visual: face parts, colours, outfits, unlocked by playing and by role-neutral milestones.
- Pro: no veteran power, cheap to defend, fits the "sculpt your disguise" idea, and the R.E.P.O. spike shows cosmetics can pull people back.
- Con: art cost per item, and no gameplay hook.
- Recommendation: make this the baseline. Keep unlocks earn-by-playing with no loss of earned rewards (Fall Guys).

**Option 2: Match-scoped mutations only, bought at the apparatus.**
- Points earned in a match buy picks from small offers (for example, one of two). Everything resets at match end.
- Pro: newcomer and veteran start equal, and it matches Stadium and R.E.P.O. practice.
- Con: needs catch-up and snowball controls (bounty-like mechanics, caps, refunds) and tuning.
- Recommendation: do this, but keep the pool tiny at first. Make the strong mutation expensive, visible and counterable, and keep point budgets low enough that one human sabotaging cannot outrun the aliens.

**Option 3: Persistent "variety pool" of sidegrade mutations (collection, not strength).**
- Meta progress adds entries to the offer pool. Each entry trades one thing for another and the pool is visible to all, so open knowledge holds.
- Whose collection counts in a lobby? Possible rules: host's, or a union (compare PEAK's group rule).
- Pro: a collection hook like DRG, no power gap if entries are true sidegrades.
- Con: overlap, trap options and a sidegrade meta (Splatoon). Highest balance burden.
- Recommendation: defer until Option 2 has data.

**Option 4: Daily "House" variant plus a weekly challenge, cosmetic rewards.**
- A shared daily seed or modifier and one weekly goal.
- Pro: a reason to return on a specific day, and talk between friends (PEAK's daily map).
- Con: needs enough layout and task variety to make days feel different, which is content-area work. I found no retention numbers.
- Recommendation: a later add-on, once there is more than one House layout.

**Option 5: Opt-in difficulty ladder for hosts.**
- Hosts raise house rules (stricter timers, more sabotage) and unlock harder ones by completing easier ones, with a cosmetic reward and a one-unlock-covers-the-lobby rule.
- Pro: veterans chase a goal without power, and it is cheap because modifiers are data.
- Con: a hidden-role game needs the ladder to be fair to both sides, and it is unclear who "completes" a ladder step when win conditions differ.
- Recommendation: hold for later.

**Option 6: Invest in moment generation instead of progression at launch.**
- Spend scope on what produces clips: voice range rules, death/respawn comedy, a short replay or clip key, spectator or respawn voice behaviour.
- Pro: it is what the wave sells on, and it is the core of our game.
- Con: not a retention loop by itself.
- Recommendation: do this in parallel with Option 1 and Option 2. Treat cosmetic drops as the planned returning-player event.

## 4. Open questions for the engineer
1. May a mutation be strong enough to change who wins (seeing through walls), or must all mutations be sidegrades with equal total value?
2. Do tasks and sabotage points both buy mutations at one price, or are they separate currencies? Should an outed human's reduced earnings be compensated?
3. Is the mutation pool for each match visible to everyone in advance, or revealed during play?
4. In a lobby, whose persistent unlocks count (host's, each player's, a union)?
5. Are face parts role-neutral by rule? Should disguise quality ever matter mechanically (as in Meccha Chameleon, where painting is the game)?
6. Is face sculpting a free-form tool or a library of parts? Who moderates shapes?
7. Must cosmetics be earned by play only, or may they be sold? The wave sells at $5.99 to $10 (table), and free-first-day worked once (Content Warning).
8. What is the minimum lobby size, and do we support public servers or friends only? Meccha Chameleon's public servers were reported messy.
9. How are humans assigned fairly across matches (Lockdown thread)?
10. What return trigger do you accept for a hobby scope: periodic content drops (the best-attested pattern) or an in-game loop (daily or weekly)?
