# The outed traitor: how hidden-role games keep a revealed villain dangerous

Tags: **[V]** verified from the cited source. **[P]** player or critic opinion. **[A]** my analysis. All Steam news links are `https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/<appid>/view/<id>`. Placeholder numbers are marked as such.

## 1. Short answer

1. Most games that survive a revealed traitor stop treating "catch the traitor" as the crew's win. The crew wins by finishing an objective, and exposure only changes how hard that is. [V] Project Winter, Dread Hunger. [A] This is the biggest lever for your design.
2. Exposure is made soft. Project Winter's "exile" locks a traitor out of the cabin's functions and does not remove them. Blood on the Clocktower (BotC) has roles whose whole job is to keep evil alive after the Demon is found.
3. The open villain gets a currency, a clock or a phase shift, not just "more kills". The currencies are Project Winter traitor credits and sabotage-earned buffs. The phase shifts are Among Us Hide n Seek "Final Hide", Goose Goose Duck (GGD) "hunts" and Deceit 2's In-Between.
4. Your identical-roles rule removes the usual lever, role abilities. The open-villain levers must come from the win-condition shape, the task and sabotage economy, and match phases.
5. Your respawn pillar removes the other usual lever. Almost every game here lets the crew kill or eject an exposed traitor. Without that, your risk is an unremovable griefer, not a doomed traitor.

## 2. Case studies

### Project Winter (closest structural match)
- **How it works [V].** Traitors win by stopping the survivors' escape without being identified or killed. They start with a red radio and a partner ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/774861/Project_Winter/)). A 2025 roadmap said Cabin Fever would be a streamlined mode. Cabin Fever launched 2025-09-18 with no rule detail in the post ([news](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/774861/view/1811138915395285)).
- **Exile is not removal [V].** An NME feature (undated, probably early-release era) says exiled players are locked out of the cabin chest, crafting table, stove and radio. The only ways to remove a traitor are killing them or escaping, so the game goes on after everyone knows ([NME](https://www.nme.com/gaming-features/bored-of-among-us-play-project-winter-2846682)).
- **Economy [V].**
  - Remote Sabotage costs 16 traitor credits. The cabin radio became sabotageable at any time because the late-only rule "often does not leave enough time". Traitor ability items (Escape Pod, Global Exile, Jam Radios, Airdrop Event) stopped being blocked during minor events ([Dec 2022](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/774861/view/5623338145769340429)).
  - Traitors gain stat buffs from trapping and sabotaging, and a proximity "information wheel" fills while they stay near survivors. The first fill shows a health bar and the second shows the role. Holding "Bleed" speeds a downed player's death timer 4x ([Nov 2022](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/774861/view/4789043687474818115)).
- **Information denial [V].** A sabotaged truth-serum syringe shows the opposite team. A player revived by another survivor returns as a basic role with no abilities ([news](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/774861/view/4598757090465375979)). The Disguise Kit lets a traitor frame others ([news](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/774861/view/4589754330732175837)).
- **Dead players [V].** Ghosts can buff the living (and dead traitors debuff them) per NME. A "Wild Spirit" ghost revenge cost 75 Ghost Power ([Feb 2024](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/774861/view/5589599109281408907)). Both Wild Spirit and the Ally role were later removed from Normal modes ([May 2025](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/774861/view/1800357164540920)). The reason is not stated.
- **What goes wrong.** The studio posted about rising player toxicity ([Jan 2023](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/774861/view/5035620840546865970)) and trialled voice moderation ([news](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/774861/view/5036749277230074794)).
- **Why it works [A].** An exposed traitor still has reach, because remote sabotage costs credits, not cover. The crew cannot simply win by pointing.

### Dread Hunger (delisted)
- **[V]** Two Thralls among eight players. The crew must escape and blow up an iceberg. "A Thrall's death does not result in an instant win for the crew" ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/dread-hunger-thrall-play-guide-winning-tips/)). Thrall spells are Cannibals, Whiteout and Spirit Walk. Totems charge the spell gauge. An exposed Thrall is advised to shift to ship sabotage and to shoot the nitroglycerin convoy.
- **[V]** The game was delisted 2023-12-01 and official servers closed 2024-01-01 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dread_Hunger)).
- **[P]** A reviewer says a suspicious Thrall "is probably going to be thrown overboard" ([GamingTrend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/dread-hunger-review-the-waters-not-as-deep-as-it-seems/)).
- **Not verified.** I could not find the rule for what happens to a revealed Thrall. The Unreal Engine developer interview returned 403.

### Among Us, including Hide n Seek
- **Hide n Seek [V].** The seeker is known to all. Crewmates do tasks to run the timer down faster and see a danger meter. In "Final Hide" the Impostor gets "a power boost, special Seek map, and a directional ping" ([official post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/945360/view/4980448574112987753)). Impostors cannot sabotage or vent in this mode ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Among_Us)).
- **Why it works [A].** The open villain is paid with a telegraphed threat and a closing clock. The post's own tone is "CRYING. SCREAMING. TERRIFIED.", which shows the fun is designed for the hunter's victims too.
- **Roles [V].** Phantom (June 2024) and Viper (Sept 2025) are Impostor roles ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Among_Us)). The Viper's designer notes say a dissolving body "promoted more discussion" and was a "push and pull" with the crew ([post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/945360/view/1811138915560834)). A new ghost role, the Influencer, sends images to the living ([Sept 2026](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/945360/view/1844751498234928)). Dead players are being given jobs.

### Goose Goose Duck
- **Open-hunt phases [V].**
  - The Raven wins as last survivor. If it reaches the final 3 it becomes invincible and astral, and a timer starts to kill everyone ([v4.02](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1568590/view/1808601382416749)).
  - The Cuckoo plants eggs. Enough hatch into a Cuckoo Hunt, and the hatchlings chase players "even through vents and hiding spots" ([v4.08](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1568590/view/1830163047255697)).
  - The crew's counter in both cases is to smash the eggs early or survive the hunt timer.
- **Snowball and redundancy [V].** The Halloween Wereduck turns eliminated villagers into Thralls. The pack gets faster and gets shorter kill cooldowns as it grows ([v4.12](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1568590/view/1845383656383544)). In the Dr. Turducken pair, the Monster cannot be voted out and the doctor's ejection loses the game for both ([v4.03](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1568590/view/1811772772449713)). The Carrier and Parasite count as one Duck for end-game maths (v4.08).
- **[A]** GGD turns "hidden role" into "open timed hunt" at a threshold. A visible counter-play window is built into the villain's power-up.

### Blood on the Clocktower
- **Dead players [V].** They lose their ability and cannot nominate. They keep one vote and still win or lose with their team ([wiki rules](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Rules_Explanation)). Evil wins if two players remain alive.
- **Exposure insurance [V].**
  - Scarlet Woman: if 5 or more players are alive and the Demon dies, she becomes the Demon ([wiki](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Scarlet_Woman)).
  - Mastermind: if the Demon is executed, play one more day, and the next execution decides the game ([wiki](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Mastermind)).
  - Evil Twin: "Good can't win if you both live" ([wiki](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Evil_Twin)).
  - Legion: executions fail if only evil voted ([wiki](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Legion)).
  - Imp: a Minion inherits the role on suicide ([wiki](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Imp)).
- **Denial and spikes [V].** The Poisoner makes one player's information unreliable each night ([wiki](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Poisoner)). The Assassin has a once-per-game kill that ignores protection ([wiki](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Assassin)).
- **[A]** BotC keeps evil alive by redundancy and conditional win rules, not by power. Its hidden-role knowledge matters far more than in your game.

### LOCKDOWN Protocol (your contrast)
- **[V]** Dissidents "stop them at all costs, without getting caught" ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2780980/LOCKDOWN_Protocol/)). The round is 8 minutes and the map is "balanced for 5-8 players" ([dev update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2780980/view/1786573930684503)). Weapons are available to everyone. The knife needed 3 frontal hits against a full-stamina target ([0.7.0](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2780980/view/1797185861741648)). A 0.15.0 rebalance aimed "to leave more space for the social aspect" ([0.15.0](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2780980/view/1811138915562759)). The defibrillator has one charge and can resurrect the dead or heavily damage the living (0.7.0).
- **[P]** A thread complains that correctly killing a dissident can get you kicked: "people think you are just randomly killing people" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2780980/discussions/0/4428814417438297109/)). A 3-player tester says that when tasks per employee are many, "the dissident just has to kill one employee to win" ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2780980/discussions/0/6546631477890117789)). Dissident requests were a knife, seeing weapons through walls, and a tablet "lights out" sabotage. There are also repeated reports of the same players being dealt dissident ([thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2780980/discussions/0/4764333414208635266/)).
- **Not verified.** Exact win and lose rules and what happens to a caught dissident. The NamuWiki page returned 403. Your account of the contrast stands as the engineer's statement.
- **[A]** This is the design your answers reject. Everyone is armed and killing is cheap, so exposure plus a weapon equals removal.

### Deceit 2 (a phase shift that may be too weak)
- **[V]** Terrors hide in the day. After activating weak points they transform in the In-Between and hunt ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deceit_2)). Innocents earn souls from tasks and need an escape key.
- **[P]** One reviewer calls the powers "lack lustre", says the Terror-form timer drains quickly, and says Truth-Seekers win "nine out of ten times" ([GGRecon](https://ggrecon.com/reviews/deceit-2-review)). That is a single critic's tally, not data.
- **[A]** A phase shift needs real teeth and a real clock. A transformation the crew can parry is a trap.

### Other hidden-role games
- **Avalon [V].** If good wins three missions, evil still gets one Assassin guess at Merlin ([rules](https://rules.dized.com/the-resistance-avalon/rule/how-to-play/ending-the-game-and-assassinating-merlin)). Spy ratios are 2 of 5, 2 of 6, 3 of 7, 3 of 8, 3 of 9 and 4 of 10 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Resistance_(game))).
- **Secret Hitler [V per search snippet of the official rules, page itself returned 403].** Fascists win if Hitler is elected Chancellor after the third Fascist policy. Presidential powers unlock as Fascist policies pass, and a veto unlocks after five ([rules](https://secrethitler.io/rules)). Escalating powers that follow the objective track are the pattern here.
- **Trouble in Terrorist Town (TTT) [V].** Traitors are about 1 in 4 players. Killing a Detective awards traitors credits, and killing a traitor awards Detectives credits ([wiki](https://troubleinterroristtown.wiki.gg/wiki/Roles)). Catch-up flows both ways.
- **The Traitors (TV) [V].** A banished player reveals their side. Remaining Traitors may recruit when one is eliminated. If any Traitor survives the end, they take the whole pot ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Traitors_(American_TV_series))).
- **Mafia [V].** Roughly one third of players are mafia, and they win at parity ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mafia_(party_game))).
- **Dubium [V].** If the Traitor dies, another Frontier may be bought over and take the role ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1234040/DUBIUM/)). Reviews are 65% positive of 336 and the game is off sale.
- **Betrayal at House on the Hill.** The traitor is revealed by the haunt and play becomes mostly one-vs-many. A critical-play piece [P] says power imbalance grows with player count and that the game is luck-driven ([article](https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2022/04/15/critical-play-betrayal-at-house-on-the-hill/)).
- **Among Shadows (unreleased, Early Access Q4 2026) [V].** Hidden Apostles raise an Infection Score, and a final Blood Moon convoy battle lets them ambush and assassinate the Lord ([Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/among-shadows-steam-early-access-q4-2026/)). It is a scripted finale where cover stops mattering.
- **Cursed Companions, Feign, Town of Salem.** I found no verifiable detail on exposed-traitor handling for these three.

### Known-villain asymmetric games
- **Dead by Daylight [V].** A generator can take at most 8 regression events (each at least 2.5% of progress). The stated goal is to end "excessively long matches", especially 3-generator standoffs ([Jan 2024](https://eip.gg/dbd/news/jan-dev-update-2024/)). A designer said "we couldn't make it feel good … to have a lot of restriction as playing as the Killer" ([GoNintendo](https://www.gonintendo.com/contents/25632-behaviour-interactive-discusses-the-challenge-of-balancing-dead-by-daylight-s-alien)). The 2v8 mode recurs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_by_Daylight)).
- **Evolve [V].** The Monster evolves in stages and is vulnerable while evolving. Reviewers disagreed on balance ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolve_(video_game))).
- **Friday the 13th [V].** Jason has time-restricted abilities (sense, teleport). Counselors win by escaping ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friday_the_13th:_The_Game)).
- **[A]** A known villain is fun when they are powerful, readable and capped. The cap exists so the villain cannot stall the game by camping.

## 3. Cross-game patterns

1. **Objective-based crew win.** Dread Hunger, Project Winter and GGD hunts all keep play going after exposure. Games where exposure ends the traitor (Lockdown, TTT) rely on weapons for everyone.
2. **Soft exposure.** Exile is a lockout. Banishment in Salem-style games is the only hard removal, and those games are built around that vote.
3. **Redundancy and succession.** Scarlet Woman, the Imp pass, Dubium, Legion, the Parasite and Wereduck thralls all say "the role survives its holder".
4. **A scheduled power spike with a counter-window.** Final Hide, the Raven and Cuckoo hunts, the haunt, the Blood Moon. Deceit 2 shows what happens when the spike is too weak.
5. **Spendable reach instead of cover.** Project Winter credits, TTT credits, remote and global sabotages.
6. **Caps on the villain.** DbD's regression cap and Project Winter's costs prevent endless stalling.
7. **Dead players get jobs.** BotC ghost vote, Project Winter ghosts, Among Us Influencer, Lockdown defibrillator.
8. **Typical ratios** are 1 in 3 to 1 in 4, and 2 of 8 is common.

## 4. Risks for your design

1. **Respawn removes removal.** Every exposed-traitor lever above assumes the crew can kill or eject. [A] With respawn, "nobody does tasks with him" is your only counter, and it is social, not mechanical. Griefing becomes the failure mode.
2. **Sabotage is a free point for everyone.** If roles are identical, an alien can sabotage and then complete a task for 2 points, the same combo as your delivery example. The only brake is the shared goal and clock. [A]
3. **Outed spiral.** Lost points mean no mutations, which means no way to compete. TTT and Project Winter both added catch-up flows for this reason. [A]
4. **Phase-shift tuning.** Too weak (Deceit 2 [P]), too strong or overpowered (Evolve [P]), or imbalance that scales with players (Betrayal [P]). With 4 to 10 players, the same numbers will not fit both ends.
5. **Task camping.** A villain guarding one task stalls the match, as in DbD's 3-generator standoffs.
6. **Harassment of the outed player.** Project Winter's toxicity posts and Lockdown's kick complaints [P]. This matters for a mixed audience.
7. **Role-deal streaks.** Lockdown players report the same people dealt dissident repeatedly [P]. Anyone who is the minority role should get a fair rotation. [A]
8. **Identity versus deduction.** Your pillar says deduction is not central, so any lever that needs secrecy to work will fail once the human is known.

## 5. Options for the engineer to decide

All numbers below are placeholders.

**Option A: Soft exile (lockout, not removal).**
- Mechanism: when a player is publicly accused or "outed", they cannot start or finish selected shared stations without a partner. The station list is a content choice.
- Trade-off: it gives the crew a real counter without killing, and fits respawn. Requires a defined "outed" event. Risks harassment and conflicts with "deduction not central".
- Borrowed from: Project Winter exile.

**Option B: Sabotage-economy as the outed human's lifeline.**
- Mechanism: sabotage points (once per task) are not affected by social exclusion. Remote or global sabotages (lights out, a siren) are bought with points. Each task allows at most N sabotage events, as DbD's cap does.
- Trade-off: the outed human keeps reach and gets catch-up income, and the cap bounds farming. Aliens could farm the same points, so pay out only when the sabotage costs the aliens something (a delay window, or a repair action by someone else).
- Borrowed from: Project Winter credits, DbD cap.

**Option C: Open-phase finale.**
- Mechanism: the last part of the match is a scheduled phase where the humans get a real, time-limited push (for example a map ping or the free use of a mutation, shared by all players and weighted by win condition) and the aliens get a visible counter-window.
- Trade-off: it makes exposure matter little and gives the "play the villain" payoff. Needs careful tuning, and Deceit 2 shows a weak phase is worse than none.
- Borrowed from: Final Hide, GGD hunts, Blood Moon.

**Option D: A last-chance win condition.**
- Mechanism: when the aliens' goal completes, humans still get one limited final objective to flip the result, such as ruining the party with accumulated sabotage points over a threshold.
- Trade-off: it directly gives "a real, hard chance even if outed" and is easy to explain. It makes matches longer and can feel cheap if the threshold is low.
- Borrowed from: Avalon Assassin, BotC Mastermind.

**Option E: Win-condition succession.**
- Mechanism: if a human leaves the match or is overwhelmed, another player can take up the humans' win condition (for example via a sabotage-point threshold).
- Trade-off: it keeps the minority alive and fits identical roles. It breaks "roles dealt privately" and may feel arbitrary. Low priority.
- Borrowed from: Dubium, BotC.

**Option F: One asymmetric rule ("Unmask").**
- Mechanism: a voluntary public reveal that grants a short buff.
- Trade-off: it makes "drop the act" a comic moment and a real power spike. It breaks the engineer's "roles mechanically identical" rule, so it needs explicit approval.

**Recommendation.** Start with B plus a light version of A, and prototype C as a stretch goal.
- B is the only option that works under identical roles, respawn and "deduction not central".
- A supplies the crew's counter that respawn removes.
- C provides the spectacle once B and A work.
- Hold D, E and F for after playtests.

## 6. Open questions

1. What is the humans' exact win condition? Is it prevent-the-aliens' goal by a clock, reach a sabotage threshold, or both? Is it team or individual?
2. Can aliens sabotage and earn sabotage points? If so, how is farming prevented?
3. What does "outed" mean mechanically? Is there an accusation or vote, a public mark, or only social knowledge?
4. What stops an outed human who cannot be killed for good? What are the respawn rules and timers?
5. Is the match timed, and what happens when the timer ends?
6. Per-task sabotage, global sabotage, or both? Who pays for global ones, and when?
7. What is the human-to-alien ratio for 4 to 10 players?
8. Does the mutation shop feed the win condition, so a points lead snowballs?
9. How much harassment protection does the mixed audience need (reporting, mute, "opt-out of being accused")?
10. How is the human role rotated across matches so the same player is not always the minority?
