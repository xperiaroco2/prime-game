## 1. The game in one line
Aliens at their prime throw the best party on Earth, at an alien lord's estate; disguised humans who hate their party
hide among them, and it all turns into chaos (GDD §1). Working title: My Alien Prime Friends.

## 2. The tone
The engineer's references are adult animated sci-fi comedies and a comic shooter with talking weapons. Their names
stay private, in his personal Claude memory (his word, #1095 comment 6199371817): the repo is public, and the M8
plan's carried-over rule keeps other works' names, designs and plots out of any brief, prompt or public text (#1039).
In the repo the tone is described, never named:
- **Adult, absurd sci-fi.** Aliens who misread suburban Earth: its parties, food, chores, neighbours and TV.
- **Tech with a personality.** Gadgets, the ship and the weapons talk, have moods, complain and get things wrong.
- **Crude, cringe, body humour.** Comic violence and flying ragdolls; never guts or dismemberment (GDD §1).
- **Earth formats redone by aliens.** The car tuning is a makeover show (#1033): the model for a task.
- **Escalation.** A small thing goes wrong in a big, dumb, clip-worthy way.
- **Not the tone:** "grandpa" humour (old films, the dacha, nostalgia), sterile industrial machines, earnest sci-fi,
  childish jokes.

**The tone test:** could this be a scene in one of the engineer's references? If not, drop it before he sees it.

**The vibe** (his six principles, approved 2026-10-16: [#1095 comment 6211564717](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game/issues/1095#issuecomment-6211564717),
from his direction in [comment 6211140103](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game/issues/1095#issuecomment-6211140103);
the examples come only from his references, never from other works):
1. **A real party, not a parody.** The aliens party hard and sincerely: they drink, smoke, dance, sing, puke and fall
   into the pool; beer, a bong, energy drinks, pizza and burgers everywhere. Adult jokes for teenagers and up. The
   tasks are woven into the party.
2. **Slay.** The aliens are masters: what they make is stylish, fashionable and modern, as on the makeover show; a job
   ends in a "wow" reveal for the whole party.
3. **Tech with a character and its own problem.** One mania or drama per device, its job taken literally to the
   absurd; the character shows in what the device does, not in lines it says (2026-10-21, below).
4. **A gadget for a small thing that blows everything up,** in front of the party.
5. **The body, the high and the gross as the joke.** High, drunk, swollen, transformed, puked on: dumb and funny,
   never scary, no guts.
6. **References everywhere.** Earth pop culture in an alien version, played straight; parody only (§3).

**Not the vibe:** a shy, childish party ("we party, we bake pies"); jokes dragged in by the ears; slow, careful work.

**A sandbox, not a show** (the engineer, 2026-10-21, [#1095 comment 6313177753](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game/issues/1095#issuecomment-6313177753);
approved: [comment 6315676554](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game/issues/1095#issuecomment-6315676554)): the fun
comes from many mechanics, moments and easter eggs that players play with; the players make the jokes, the game never
scripts them or leads by the hand.

## 3. The pillars as a checklist
Each idea answers these (GDD §1 is the source; numbers are placeholders until a balance model and a playtest):
- Fun from the first second; never boring: nobody waits long, a mistake costs a short time out.
- Randomness makes the stories, and always leaves a chance to react.
- The party is hard on its own: aliens who understand nothing fail without any humans.
- The humans spoil deniably, hidden in the noise of honest mistakes.
- Voice first; every task can still be done alone, slower; enough tasks need no voice.
- Action over long discussions; death costs only time.
- Macro skill first; aim may matter a little.
- Open knowledge: every rule is known to everyone.
- Nothing can be hidden that cannot be made again: printed, grown or taken from a pool; hiding costs only time
  (the engineer, 2026-10-20, [#1095 comment 6289528171](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game/issues/1095#issuecomment-6289528171)).
- References as parody, never another work's names, logos, characters or assets.

## 4. What went wrong before
- **A hidden number found by tests turns into brute force** ("put the strongest in everywhere, then take them away"),
  however it is dressed (#1033, the engine, 2026-10-15). Look for fun in an action, a joke or people, not a search.
- **Take a principle from Lockdown Protocol, never its expression;** a one-to-one reskin or its chores (containers
  to wash) are out (#1033).
- **One resource across many tasks stifles:** the plants feed at most three mechanics, the engine lightly (#1033).
- **The tone came from the session, not the game:** ideas from old films and a dacha (#1120, the intervention entry
  of 2026-10-15).
- **Too much at once:** four detailed variants with tables lost him; maths before the fun is backwards.
- **Written gags:** six cards of jokes for the MVP's tasks (a host's voice, talking devices, reveal lines) were
  rejected as "some stand-up show": the game must be fun from being in it and from what its models are like
  ([#1095 comment 6313151159](https://github.com/xperiaroco2/prime-game/issues/1095#issuecomment-6313151159)).

## 5. How an idea moves
1. **One-liners:** 5 to 8 ideas, one line each, each past the tone test; no numbers, no tables.
2. **He picks** one, mixes some or rejects all; a rejection is information: ask what missed.
3. **Expand the pick:** the fantasy, the player's steps, what others see and hear, the humans' deniable
   spoils, the clip moment, what it needs from the map.
4. **Check it:** holes, fairness, balance, the numbers as placeholders. The careful thinking belongs here, not in
   step 1, where it turns ideas cautious.
5. **Record:** his words on the issue in English, marked as translated, idea or decided; a decision into the GDD or
   `docs/design/` on his word; ideas into `ideas/` and research into `research/` of the private design repo
   prime-game-design (`D:\prime-game-design`; #1124).
